KUMIKO FRAME — 460 x 240 mm, pattern: Asanoha (hemp leaf)
Cell 30 mm · grid bar 2.4 x 6 mm · infill bar 1.6 x 6 mm · border 10 mm x 7 mm
Seams: zigzag along the lattice (half-width bars on each side)

TILES: 2 columns x 1 rows = 2 prints
Rows are lettered from the top (A = top row), columns numbered from the left. See layout.svg.

PRINT
- 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 mm layers. Brim 5 mm (or brim ears).
- Bars should print solid: enough wall loops to fill each bar width.
- PLA or PETG on textured PEI.
- STLs contain overlapping bars. Bambu Studio, Orca and PrusaSlicer merge them; if not, use repair / merge parts.

ASSEMBLE
1. Dry-fit all tiles face down on a flat surface, using layout.svg.
2. Neighboring tiles nest along their zigzag edges.
3. Frame: 6 separate pieces with 18 snap pegs on their backing lattice. The outer tiles' hidden hub holes press onto these pegs: each peg is split with a small barb at the tip, the same click-lock as the lap teeth, so tiles snap in from the front and pop out with a firm pull. Frame STLs are flipped so the backing lattice and pegs print flat on the bed.
4. Mark the wall: print wall_template.svg at 100% (actual size), tape it up with its top line level, and drill the red crosses (one per tile puck) and the 6 orange crosses (one per frame standoff). Use #6 pan-head screws with anchors.
5. Frame: screw the 6 frame standoffs to the wall over the orange boxes, long side along the frame edge, pegs pointing out. Press each frame piece onto its standoffs until the pegs click. The standoffs sit entirely behind the frame bar and each screw head tucks into a pocket in the frame's back, so nothing shows. The frame stands 5 mm off the wall.
6. Tiles: screw a round puck over each blue circle (the screw goes down the middle of the hollow peg). Press each tile in: it pushes onto its two puck pegs, and outer tiles also click onto the frame's snap pegs. Each puck hides completely behind the round hub on the back of its tile. Zigzag edges nest with the neighbors.
7. Level the seams: lay a straightedge across each seam. If one tile sits lower than its neighbor (walls are rarely flat), loosen that puck's screw a turn, slide a shim in behind it (the slot fits around the screw), and retighten. Notches on the shims mark thickness: 1 = 0.4 mm, 2 = 0.8 mm, 3 = 1.2 mm. Stack them if needed.
8. Swap or add later: pull any tile straight out (outer tiles release from the frame with a firm pull) and press another in. The frame stays on the wall. Tiles float 6 mm off the wall.
