## Product direction (Oct 17): modular cells
Base model = frame + bare triangle grid in one color. Every cell takes a snap-in **backer chip** (color) and an **insert piece** (asanoha / mitsukude / none), placed from the front, so customers redesign their own panel. Sold as kits: color-sorted bags + numbered map (paint-by-numbers). Add-ons priced separately.
- `cli/cell-coupon.js` makes the fit test (two-cell base with a stepped back ledge, chips + inserts at tight/medium/snug). **Next geometry task once Travis reports the winning fit:** build the cell system into the engine (ledge in every cell of bare tiles, per-cell chip and insert exports cut to partial edge cells, per-cell placement from the photo designer), replacing the per-tile backer.
- Risks: ~350 cells per Classic = up to ~700 small parts (kit, not pre-assembled); low resolution (bold simple images only); customer-image copyright (terms + refusal of brands/characters).

## Ground rules from Travis (in addition to AGENTS.md)
- **One codebase.** Don't paste in builders or engines generated by other AI tools; earlier ones were truncated, reintroduced visible wall plates and dropped the tests. Port an idea into this engine with a test instead.
- Never call the product "traditional Japanese kumiko". Patterns are centuries-old Japanese designs; the system is original.
- Marketing and listing images must show real printed parts. AI imagery is mood only.
- "KALLAX" only as "fits IKEA KALLAX", never in a product name.
